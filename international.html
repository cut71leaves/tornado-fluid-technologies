Suspended Cavitation Technology. Research, engineering and sector applications.

About Tornado Fluid

We are a technology company focused on suspended cavitation. We develop new approaches to cavitation generation and evaluate their applications across food and beverages, chemicals and environmental processes.

From research to engineering

A working demonstrator has been built and comparative experiments have been conducted. Application suitability and performance must be assessed against specific media, operating conditions and process requirements.

Areas of focus

Alcoholic beverage refinement is a key application. Cutting fluids and water treatment are additional focus areas, alongside longer-term opportunities in personal care, pharmaceuticals and energy.
